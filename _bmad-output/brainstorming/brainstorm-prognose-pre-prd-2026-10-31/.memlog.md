- (technique) started Morphological Analysis
- (insight by coach) The period unit should follow the ordering cycle, not just the weekly review cycle; period unit drives seasonal length (12 monthly vs 52 weekly) and number of data points for 2-3 years of synthetic data (24-36 vs 104-156)
- (decision by user) Period unit is week (orders are placed weekly); forecast horizon is the next 3 weeks to plan purchasing
- (insight by coach) Weekly period implies season length 52; 52 weeks of history shows one season only (cannot verify seasonal pattern), 104 weeks allows a simple seasonal index and a check against a repeat year
- (question) How many weeks of history in the synthetic data (52, 104, 156)? Then: does seasonality enter the calculation or only appear in the data and chart (brief lists only MA and exponential smoothing)?
- (decision by user) Synthetic data: 104 weeks (2 years) of history per product as the rule, so seasonal variation can be compared between two years without overcomplicating; plus some test products with shorter history
- (insight by coach) Short-history test products double as test cases for the low-confidence badge and the too-little-data warning
- (idea by coach) Spark: simple seasonal index applied on top of either method (divide history by week index, forecast, multiply back), keeping calculations hand-checkable
- (question) Does seasonality change the forecast numbers (via a seasonal index) or only show in data and chart?
- (decision by user) Seasonal variation must affect the forecast calculation itself, not just the chart; use a simple seasonal adjustment that is easy to understand and check; stay within school-project scope
- (question) Exact seasonal method (index per week vs per month group, how the index is estimated from 2 years, interaction with outlier and stockout exclusion) to be validated in research
- (technique) started Worst Possible Idea
- (idea by coach) Spark (worst dataset): product with 0 sales in 103 weeks and one week of 5000 (one-off bulk order); median is 0 so the outlier rule 2x median flags everything above 0
- (idea by user) Worst case: product normally sells 100/week but is out of stock for 4 weeks; system records 0 sales and computes a far too low forecast; system must distinguish zero sales due to no demand from zero sales due to stockout
- (insight by coach) Edge case chain: after 4 stockout weeks, the first normal week (100) has median of 3 preceding weeks = 0, so the 2x median outlier rule flags it unless stockout weeks are excluded before the outlier check; rule: outlier median uses the 3 preceding valid (non-stockout) weeks
- (question) With a 4-week stockout gap, should the 3-week moving average use the last 3 valid weeks (skip over the gap, possibly stale) or only the valid weeks inside the last 3 calendar weeks (window shrinks, maybe empty)?
- (decision by user) Moving average skips stockout weeks and uses the last 3 valid weeks; if valid data is old or history is too short, system shows an uncertainty warning
- (question) Parked: numeric threshold for 'old' valid data (e.g. 8 weeks?) and for 'too little history'; feeds the confidence badge rules
- (insight by coach) Consecutive spikes (100 then 300, 320, 310): with the 2x median rule over 3 preceding weeks, first two are flagged but third is not, so a real level shift is half-excluded; need a rule for runs of high weeks
- (idea by coach) Spark: flag only isolated spikes (1-2 weeks); 3+ consecutive high weeks count as a new demand level, shown as an info note, not excluded
- (decision by user) Sales that stay at a higher level for 3+ weeks are treated as a possible new normal level, not as outliers; forecast takes the increase into account and shows a message that demand has changed
- (question) Remaining edge cases: outlier check in first 3 weeks, median 0 without stockout, safety margin on which scenario, rounding of order quantity; and does order quantity cover 1 week or the 3-week horizon?
- (decision by user) Order suggestion covers next week only, including safety margin and available stock; forecasts for weeks 2 and 3 are used for further planning, not summed into one order
- (idea by coach) Spark (proposals to confirm): order quantity never below 0 (if stock exceeds need, show 0 and an overstock note); round up to whole units; weeks 2-3 forecast can warn when stock will not last
- (question) Which scenario (optimistic/normal/pessimistic) drives the order quantity by default, and is the safety margin added on top of that scenario?
- (decision by user) Order suggestion is based on the normal scenario by default, with safety margin added on top; optimistic and pessimistic are shown as alternatives so the user can compare them
- (question) Confirm coach proposals: order quantity floored at 0 with overstock note; rounded up to whole units
- (decision by user) Order quantity never negative: shows 0 when stock covers demand; rounded up to whole units; pack sizes not considered in v1
- (idea by coach) Spark: minimum-history rules: no forecast below the moving-average window of valid weeks; seasonal adjustment only when enough history (e.g. 104 weeks), otherwise non-seasonal forecast with a note; outlier check only starts once 3 valid preceding weeks exist
- (question) Numeric thresholds: minimum valid weeks to show a forecast, history needed for seasonal adjustment, and what counts as 'old' valid data and 'too little data' for low confidence
- (decision by user) Thresholds: forecast shown only with at least 3 valid weeks; uncertainty warning if latest valid sales data is older than 8 weeks; fewer than 12 valid weeks gives low confidence; keep rules simple and testable

- (decision by user) Confidence levels accepted: high = 104+ valid weeks and no active warnings; medium = 12-103 valid weeks or active notes; low = under 12 valid weeks
- (decision by user) If chosen moving-average window exceeds the number of valid weeks, show an insufficient-data message and do not compute the forecast with that window; keep it simple
