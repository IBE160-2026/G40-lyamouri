Prognose as a calm, checkable worksheet. The product overview is one dense table that fits about 30 products on an office screen, and the product screen reads top to bottom like a hand calculation: weeks used → forecast → scenarios → order calculation line, every figure with its formula. Near-monochrome with one teal accent, hairline rules, tabular numerals, no motion: the register is the accountant's ledger, built for “can I check this by hand?”.
