Konsept B – «Innkjøpsmøtet»

Prognose as a coach for the 20 minutes before the Monday purchasing meeting (UJ-1). The overview opens with “this week” in four numbers and a three-step path to the combined order overview, and each product is a card with the one number to order in large type. The product screen answers three plain questions in order: what is happening with sales, can I trust the forecast, and what should I order. Roomy and friendly, with large type, a pale sage canvas and ochre actions: the register is a helpful colleague, not a spreadsheet.
