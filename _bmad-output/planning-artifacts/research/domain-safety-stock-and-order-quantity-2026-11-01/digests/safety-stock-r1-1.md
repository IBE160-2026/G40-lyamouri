## Worked example(s) / formulas (only as supported by the rows above)
- Safety stock, continuous/lead-time form: SS = z x sigma_per_period x sqrt(L) (row 1; example z 1.65, sigma 20, L 3 gives about 57).
- Periodic review form: protection period = T + L; S = mean demand per period x (T + L) + z x sigma x sqrt(T + L) (rows 6, 7).
- Order each review: Q = max(0, S - inventory position), inventory position = on-hand + on-order - backorders (row 8); NC State version Q = R - I (row 4).
- Rounding: round Q up to pack size, e.g. 64 -> 70 for packs of 10 (row 9).
- Coverage: weeks of cover = stock / forecast (or average) demand per week (row 11).
- Relation to the user's formula (analysis, not a sourced claim): the proposed "next-week forecast + safety% - stock on hand" is the (R,S) formula with T = 1 week, L = 0 (so protection period = 1 week), safety stock expressed as a percentage instead of z x sigma x sqrt(T+L), and inventory position replaced by on-hand only (equal when nothing is on order). It matches row 4/6/8 structurally. If L >= 1 period or orders are open, rows 6 and 8 imply the target must cover T + L and subtract on-order stock.
