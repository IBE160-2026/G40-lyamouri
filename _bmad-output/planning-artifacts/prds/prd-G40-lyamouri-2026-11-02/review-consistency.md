# Consistency review: Prognose PRD

**Scope.** `prd.md` and `addendum.md` in this folder. No other file was opened, so the external paths in PRD §0 and addendum §6 were not checked. Severity is High, Medium or Low. "c1" means an FR's first testable consequence.

**Verdict.** The PRD is well built and can go to UX design and architecture once the High finding and the Medium findings are fixed. Every numbered ID and § reference points to an existing item; six references claim more than their target holds. §4 and §8 agree on every FR's priority. E1 to E14 and the addendum's weighted scores recompute correctly. The High finding blocks the Must gate. Most Medium findings come from a missing precedence rule for the confidence label, two undefined terms ("active warning", "note"), and drift in what "base forecast" and "level shift" mean.

## 1. Glossary discipline

"Current stock" never appears, and "message" never stands for a warning: FR-1, FR-20, FR-29 and NFR-5 use it only for error and validation messages. Norwegian UI strings are treated as intentional; one is noted at the end of this section and not counted.

- **GL-1 · Medium · §4 to §11.** Quote: "product overview", "product screen", "note", "active warning", "latest valid week", "week-1 normal forecast", "calculation line", "outlier rule", "baseline", "seasonal adjustment", "window", "smoothing factor", "test product", "approved". Problem: these domain nouns have no §3 row. The ones shown in the UI (product overview, product screen, note, window, smoothing factor, the unusual-week setting, approved) have no Norwegian label, although §3 says "Both are binding". Fix: add §3 rows with Norwegian labels for the UI terms, and define "active warning", "note", "latest valid week" and "week-1 normal forecast".
- **GL-2 · Low · FR-24.** Quote: "week-1 base forecast". Problem: §3 (Safety margin, Weeks of cover, Suggested order quantity), FR-11, R-8, R-12, E5, E6 and E13 all say "week-1 normal forecast". Fix: use "week-1 normal forecast".
- **GL-3 · Low · §1, FR-9 c3, FR-11 c2, FR-15 c3, FR-21 c2, FR-33.** Quote: "forecast plus safety margin minus stock on hand"; "covers the forecast plus the safety margin"; "gets the same forecast"; "the affected forecast"; "the forecast for weeks 2 and 3". Problem: bare "forecast" is used where "week-1 normal forecast" (§1, FR-11) or "base forecast" (FR-9, FR-15, FR-21, FR-33) is meant. Fix: use the glossary term.
- **GL-4 · Low · UJ-1, §1, SM-7, §3 Warning, §4.1, §7, FR-3 c3, FR-14, §2.2.** Quote: "the next three weeks"; "worked example"; "every confidence level"; "latest valid data"; "the seed script"; "stock figures"; "covers demand", "next week's demand", "covers the need"; "MRP planning" (§2.2) against "MRP II functions" (§7). Problem: these are synonyms for forecast horizon, reference example, confidence label, latest valid week, generator script, stock on hand, and week-1 normal forecast plus safety margin. MRP and MRP II are different things. Fix: use the glossary terms and pick one of MRP or MRP II.
- **GL-5 · Low · §3 Settings, FR-20, FR-16, FR-17 c1, SM-3; §3 Forecasting method.** Quote: "handling of unusual weeks", "Unusual weeks", "the company setting", "the unusual-week setting"; "factor 0.1 to 0.9". Problem: one setting has four names, and "factor" stands in for "smoothing factor". Fix: pick one name (for example "unusual-week handling") and use "smoothing factor" throughout.
- **GL-6 · Low · §4.6, §4.9, FR-29, FR-31.** Quote: "[persona] can replace the suggested order quantity". Problem: the persona name is the actor in some FRs, while FR-1 and FR-8 say "user", and "user" has no §3 row. Fix: write "the user" in FRs and keep the persona in UJ-1.
- **GL-7 · Low · §3 Week, FR-7, R-14, E1, E12.** Quote: "a calendar week identified by year and week number"; "Latest data week 104". Problem: "week 1" is a horizon week in FR-7, a week number in R-14 and a position in the data in E1 ("weeks 1 to 8") and E12. Fix: write "horizon week 1 to 3", and "week index n" in the examples.
- *Not counted:* FR-12 «Prognose 100 … − lager 40» uses «Prognose» and «lager» where §3 has «Grunnprognose» and «Lagerbeholdning», and §3 says "no synonyms in either language". Confirm that the short forms are deliberate, or add them to §3.

## 2. Cross-references

All other references resolve and say what the citing text claims, including every reference from the addendum to the PRD. All seven `[ASSUMPTION]` tags are indexed in §11.

- **XR-1 · Medium · §4 intro, AG-1, NFR-2, SM-1.** Quote: "Formulas, conventions and reference examples for all calculations are in §5"; "warnings and confidence labels come only from the rules in §5". Problem: §5 has no rule for the confidence label (it is only in §3 and FR-19) or for the old-data warning (only in FR-18, although E12 in §5.6 tests it). NFR-2 therefore does not require either rule to be in the calculation module. SM-1 says it "Validates … FR-19", but no E-n covers a confidence label or the FR-12 line. Fix: add R-15 (old-data warning) and R-16 (confidence label with precedence, see IC-3) to §5, plus a reference example for the confidence label.
- **XR-2 · Medium · FR-9 c3, FR-17 c3, §1.** Quote: "matches its reference example in §5"; "The level-shift test product (FR-3) gives the forecast in its reference example"; "check each forecast against a worked example". Problem: §5.6 has no example for either 104-week test product. E14 uses a 4-week toy cycle and E9 a 6-week toy series. Fix: put the test products' expected values in the reference spreadsheet (DR-3) and cite them, or reword to "the rule reproduces E14" and "E9".
- **XR-3 · Low · FR-6 c2.** Quote: "the exponential-smoothing reference examples in §5". Problem: only E2 exists, and no exponential-smoothing example skips a stockout week or an excluded week (FR-6 c1). Fix: cite E2 and add one such example.
- **XR-4 · Low · §4.4.** Quote: "Realizes UJ-1 steps 2 and 4." Problem: adjusting (step 4) is FR-29 in §4.9, which is Should; §4.4 is Must and realizes step 2 and the climax. Fix: "Realizes UJ-1 step 2 and the climax."
- **XR-5 · Low · §4.6, FR-21.** Quote: "Realizes the teacher's core flow." Problem: within the PRD, the core flow is defined only in SM-3. Fix: "Realizes the core flow (SM-3)."
- **XR-6 · Low · SM-7.** Quote: "Every warning and every confidence level is triggered … Validates FR-3, FR-15 to FR-19." Problem: the insufficient-data warnings come from FR-8, which is not listed. "Every warning" also covers the Should level-shift warning and the Could FR-33 warning. Fix: add FR-8 and name the warnings in scope.

## 3. Priorities

FR-1 to FR-34 have the same priority in §4 and in §8: 23 Must, 6 Should plus the FR-17 warning, and 5 Could. The UJ-1 priority note matches FR-29 and FR-30 (Should) and FR-31 (Could).

- **PR-1 · High · SM-1, NFR-2, DR-3 against DR-6, SM-C2.** Quote: "100% of the reference examples E1 to E14 pass … Validates FR-5 to FR-12 … and rules R-1 to R-14"; "No Should or Could item is started before the Must scope passes SM-1 and SM-3." Problem: E14, R-14 and FR-9 are Should. The Must scope cannot pass SM-1 until a Should item is built, and no Should item may start until the Must scope passes SM-1. Fix: make the Must gate E1 to E13, R-1 to R-13, FR-5 to FR-8, FR-10 to FR-12 and FR-15 to FR-19. Check E14, R-14 and FR-9 when FR-9 is built, and move FR-9 c4 (the flat-forecast test) to FR-7.
- **PR-2 · Medium · FR-17, §8.2 against §3 Level shift, Warning, Confidence label.** Quote: "Should for the warning"; "reported with a warning"; "Medium: … or any active warning". Problem: §3 does not mark the level-shift warning as Should, yet the Must confidence label depends on it. A level-shift product with 104 valid weeks is High in the Must build and becomes Medium once the Should warning ships. FR-26 (Must) already explains the level shift. Fix: make the warning Must, or state that a level shift counts as an active warning for the label whether or not the warning text exists, and mark §3 to match.
- **PR-3 · Low · headings of §4.3 to §4.6, §4.8, §4.9.** Quote: "4.3 Forecast Calculation (Must)". Problem: sections tagged "(Must)" contain Should FRs (FR-9, FR-13, FR-14, the FR-17 warning) and a Could FR (FR-23). §4.8 and §4.9 have no tag. Fix: tag mixed sections as mixed, or drop the section tags.
- **PR-4 · Low · UJ-1 edge cases and priority note.** Quote: "the suggested order quantity is 0 with an overstock note". Problem: the overstock note is FR-14, which is Should, and the priority note does not say so. Fix: add "The overstock note is Should."
- **PR-5 · Low · DR-1.** Quote: "how to switch on the optional language model and what happens without it". Problem: DR-1 is Must (§8.1) but depends on FR-28, which is Should. Fix: add "if FR-28 is built".

## 4. Testability

- **TE-1 · Medium · FR-16 c1, c4.** Quote: "always marked clearly in the chart"; "shows clearly which weeks were excluded". Problem: "clearly" cannot be checked. Fix: c1 "each unusual week has its own marker shape, explained in the legend (NFR-7)"; c4 "the screen lists every excluded week by year and week number and shows the number of calculation weeks used".
- **TE-2 · Medium · FR-21 c1, c2.** Quote: "states clearly"; "Changing any setting visibly changes the affected forecast, scenarios or suggested order quantity". Problem: "visibly" cannot be checked, and "any setting" is false. The smoothing factor has no effect while moving average is chosen, the window has none under exponential smoothing, and the unusual-week setting has none for a product without unusual weeks. Fix: c1 "shows the text «Endringen gjelder alle produkter i bedriften.»"; c2 "after a change, the product screen shows values recalculated with the new settings without a manual reload; for the E1 history, changing the window from 3 to 2 changes the base forecast from 14,3 to 13,5".
- **TE-3 · Medium · FR-27.** Quote: "shown in its own clearly marked area, apart from the calculated numbers". Problem: the only consequence is about language-model text, which needs FR-28 (Should), so the Must part has no test. Fix: add "the explanation is a separate element headed «Forklaring», and neither the calculation line nor the chart is inside it".
- **TE-4 · Low · FR-10 c3.** Quote: "presented as what-if alternatives, not as probabilities". Problem: there is nothing to observe. Fix: "the scenarios are labelled normal, optimistisk and pessimistisk; no probability is shown; a fixed note says they are what-if alternatives".
- **TE-5 · Low · FR-28 c4.** Quote: "does not answer in time". Problem: no timeout is given, and the consequence ignores NFR-8's 2-second target. Fix: say "within the timeout set in the architecture", and state whether the template explanation is shown first and replaced only when the rephrased text passes the check.
- **TE-6 · Low · FR-13, FR-14, FR-22, FR-23, FR-30 to FR-34.** Quote: §0 "each with testable consequences". Problem: these FRs have no consequences. FR-30 gives nothing to test (sort order, products without a forecast), and FR-33's "will not cover the forecast for weeks 2 and 3" has two readings. Fix: add one or two consequences to each, starting with FR-22 (Must) and FR-30. Define FR-33 as stock on hand + suggested order quantity < the base forecasts for weeks 1, 2 and 3 added together.
- **TE-7 · Low · FR-20 c1.** Quote: "A value outside the allowed range is refused". Problem: the step and precision of each setting are not given (is 12.5% allowed? is 0.25?), and NFR-5 shows whole percentages, so boundary tests have no expected result. Fix: add a precision column, for example whole weeks, one decimal for the smoothing factor and whole percentages.
- **TE-8 · Low · FR-25 c2.** Quote: "each kind looks different". Problem: this is vague, and NFR-7 already has a testable version. Fix: "differ by more than colour and are explained in the legend (NFR-7)".

## 5. Internal contradictions

These pairs agree: the §3 defaults and ranges with FR-20 and §11; the FR-19 thresholds (12 and 104 valid weeks) with §3, §11 and the FR-3 test-product bands; FR-18 ("more than 8 weeks") with E12; R-10 and R-11 with §11.

- **IC-1 · Medium · §3 Base forecast against R-14 step 3, E14.** Quote: "the same value for weeks 1, 2 and 3 unless seasonal adjustment is added"; "calculate the base forecast on these adjusted values … and multiply the result by the index"; "adjusted base forecast 20.216. Forecast for weeks 1 to 3: 10.982 …". Problem: in §3 the base forecast is the seasonally adjusted value for each week, which feeds R-7 and R-8. R-14 and E14 use "base forecast" for the deseasonalised 20.216. Fix: call 20.216 the "deseasonalised forecast" and write "base forecast for weeks 1 to 3: 10.982, 18.968, 29.950".
- **IC-2 · Medium · FR-12 c2 against R-9.** Quote: "Every number on the line equals the value used in the calculation"; "Calculations use full precision … shown with one decimal". Problem: for E4 the line shows 14,3 while the calculation uses 14.333. The numbers shown can also fail to add up: with a week-1 normal forecast of 10.04 and a stock on hand of 11, the line shows 10,0 + 1,0 − 11 but the result is 1. R-9 sets no display precision for the margin amount or for weeks of cover (E13 shows 0.35). Fix: write "equals the value used, rounded for display per R-9", set the display precision for the margin amount and weeks of cover, and say what the line shows when the rounded numbers do not add up.
- **IC-3 · Medium · §3 Confidence label, FR-19 c1 and c3, FR-16 c5.** Quote: "Low: fewer than 12 valid weeks. Medium: 12 to 103 valid weeks, or any active warning"; "A product without a forecast (FR-8) is labelled low"; "switching the setting does not change the confidence label by itself". Problem: the rule has no precedence: a product with 5 valid weeks and a stockout warning is both Low and Medium. The no-forecast rule is missing from §3 and clashes with c1. Take a product with 12 valid weeks, one of them an excluded unusual week, and window 12: it has 11 calculation weeks and no forecast, so c3 makes it Low and c1 makes it Medium, and here switching the setting does change the label. Fix: use one ordered rule: Low if fewer than 12 valid weeks or no forecast; otherwise Medium if fewer than 104 valid weeks or any active warning; otherwise High. Qualify FR-16 c5.
- **IC-4 · Medium · §3 Level shift, FR-17 against R-11.** Quote: "Three or more consecutive high weeks"; "three or more consecutive weeks that each break the outlier rule"; "Three or more unusual weeks in a row (counting valid weeks only)". Problem: when a stockout week falls inside the run (20, 22, 21, 50, stockout, 52, 51), R-11 finds a level shift and "consecutive" does not. "High weeks" is not defined. Fix: use R-11's wording in §3 and FR-17, and add a reference example with a stockout week inside the run.
- **IC-5 · Medium · §3 Warning against FR-33, FR-7, FR-9, FR-14, R-14.** Quote: "A plain-language message about the data behind a forecast"; "The system warns when the stock on hand plus the suggested order quantity will not cover". Problem: §3 lists five warnings, all about data, and the label drops to Medium on "any active warning". FR-33 adds a stock warning that is not on the list, and the notes in FR-7, FR-9, FR-14 and R-14 are not classed as either. A Could stock warning could therefore lower the confidence label. Fix: define "active warning" as the five §3 warnings only. Add "note" to §3 and make FR-33 a note, or state that it does not affect the label.
- **IC-6 · Low · FR-24 against R-12.** Quote: "The product overview also shows weeks of cover". Problem: FR-24's list of what the overview shows leaves out weeks of cover. Fix: add weeks of cover to FR-24.
- **IC-7 · Low · FR-28, DR-1 against §3 Settings, FR-20.** Quote: "When switched on". Problem: the language-model switch is not one of the six settings. It is unclear whether it is a company setting (which would need an FR-20 row and FR-2 isolation) or local configuration in `.env.example`. Fix: state which.
- **IC-8 · Low · §3 Explanation, FR-27 c1, AG-3, AG-4.** Quote: "labelled as AI-written text"; "Text produced or rephrased"; "only rephrases"; "Text written or rephrased". Problem: "written" and "produced" contradict "only rephrases", and do not match the label «KI-omformulert forklaring». Fix: say "rephrased" throughout.
- **IC-9 · Low · §11.** Quote: "old-data warning after 8 weeks". Problem: the wording is unclear at exactly 8 weeks. FR-18 and E12 say more than 8, so 8 weeks gives no warning. Fix: "more than 8 weeks".
- **IC-10 · Low · UJ-1 resolution and edge cases against FR-29, FR-30, §3 Combined order overview.** Quote: "an order quantity decided for every product"; "replace the suggested order quantity". Problem: a product without a forecast has no suggestion to replace and no marking in the combined order overview, yet in UJ-1 the user decides it by hand. Fix: allow an adjusted order quantity without a suggestion, and define how it is marked.
- **IC-11 · Low · FR-1 c3, NFR-3, DR-2 against FR-3 c5, DR-1.** Quote: "No plain-text password exists in the database, the seed data or the logs"; "no secrets are committed". Problem: the README has to list the test passwords in plain text, and the generator script probably contains them too. Fix: state that the test-company passwords are public demo credentials, not secrets, and that only their hashes are stored.
